Cook it. Plate it. Serve it. Feed Max.

How to Play

1
Tap to cook

Tap the Fridge or Pantry to grab ingredients. Ariel walks there by herself. Glowing stations show where to go next.

2
Plate & serve

Take finished food to the Plating island, then tap the family member who ordered it. The Serving Pass holds extra plates.

3
Feed Max!

When Max gets loose, tap him. Pick the snack he wants, then chase him down before he wrecks the kitchen.

Ariel carries one thing at a time. Don't let food burn, and don't let the family melt down: three meltdowns and the round is over.
