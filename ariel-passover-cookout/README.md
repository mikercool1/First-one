# Ariel Passover Cookout

A very simple tap game for little kids, made for a phone held upright. It works anywhere and has no build step: just open `index.html`.

- A family member (Ikey, Simon, Cari, Nana, Michael or Sarah) sits down and shows the dish they want.
- Tap the matching picture out of the three at the bottom. Ariel runs it over and they eat happily.
- A wrong tap just gets a "Try again!" and that picture fades out. There's no timer and no way to lose.
- Max toddles in twice wanting a snack: a cookie, apple, sippy cup or matzah.
- Serve 10 and Passover is saved. You get 3 stars for no mistakes, 2 for three or fewer, and 1 otherwise.

`art.js` draws the people and the food. `game.js` runs the game.
