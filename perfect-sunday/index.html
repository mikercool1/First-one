      HOW TO PLAY

      1. Roll the die and move.
      2. Land on a space and flip its card.
      3. Gain or lose Happiness.
      4. First to 10 Happiness has the perfect Sunday.

        Every friend has a passive that's always on and a ★ special you can use once. When a card is about to hurt, the game asks if you want to use it.
