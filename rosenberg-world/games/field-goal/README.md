# Split the Uprights

Field goals, nothing else. Pick Reuben, Jonah, Ellie or Max and kick from behind them in a 2.5D stadium.

- **Tap 1** locks your aim (the arrow on the grass swings left and right).
- **Tap 2** locks your power. Get the marker above the dashed REACH line or the ball won't clear the crossbar.
- Every make moves the kick back 3 yards (22 up to 62) and the wind gets stronger (watch the red ribbons on the uprights).
  From level 3 the ball can sit on either hash. Day, golden hour, night and snow take turns every 3 levels.
- 3 footballs. A miss costs one and you retry the same kick. Every fifth level you clear gives one back.

Files: `index.html` (screens and styles) and `game.js` (camera, stadium, physics, sounds). The kids are drawn by
Rosenberg World's own `../../js/art.js`, so they look exactly like they do in the world.

Inside Rosenberg World it's the football field in the Sports Complex (tap the goalposts or the FIELD GOALS sign).
At game over it reports one star per field goal made (up to 10). Add `?debug` to the URL to get `window.__S`.
