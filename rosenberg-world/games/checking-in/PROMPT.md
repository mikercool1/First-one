# Prompt: "Checking In" — front desk at Baha Mar

Create a new game called Checking In.

- It takes place in Rosenberg World, on Baha Mar island.
- It's a 2.5D game.
- Guests line up at the front desk and you have to check them in before they get
  angry and leave the hotel.
- Each guest is checking into a different hotel, so you need to give them the right
  number of bracelets. The bracelets are their room keys, and the color tells you
  which hotel:
  - **Gray** band: Rosewood
  - **Blue** band: Grand Hyatt
  - **Pink** band: SLS
- Rosewood guests also get a welcome beverage.

## How it plays
- One bracelet per guest in the party, in the hotel's color.
- Rosewood parties get a welcome drink for every guest too.
- Ring **Check in** to hand everything over. A wrong color, wrong count or a
  missing drink costs patience and the guest tells you what's wrong.
- Everyone in line has a patience meter. When it runs out they storm out.
  Three walkouts and your shift is over.
- Shifts get busier: bigger parties, shorter patience, faster arrivals. From shift 3
  the bins only show colors, so you have to know which color goes with which hotel.
- Rosenberg World VIPs show up now and then and tip double.

Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 bands, 4 or D drink, Backspace undo, Enter check in.
