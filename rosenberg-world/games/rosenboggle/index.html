Rosenboggle

Shake the dice, then find as many words as you can before time runs out.

  • Drag from letter to letter. Each next letter has to touch the last one, even diagonally.
  • Words need 3 or more letters. You can't use the same die twice in one word.
  • Or tap letters one at a time, then tap Enter word. On a keyboard you can just type.
  • Points: 3–4 letters 1, 5 letters 2, 6 letters 3, 7 letters 5, 8+ letters 11.
