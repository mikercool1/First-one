# Coco Party

A hot chocolate stand game, made for a phone held upright. Open `index.html`; there's no build step.
It plays exactly like Frozenbergs (the ice cream game), with a snowy winter skin.

- Pick who's making cocoa: Reuben, Jonah or Ellie.
- A customer walks up and their order hangs at the top of the screen.
- Tap **Make the cocoa!** The builder fills the screen: pick the cup, the cocoa and the toppings, one step at a time.
  - Cups: Mug, Paper Cup, Glass Mug
  - Cocoa: Hot Chocolate or White Hot Chocolate
  - Toppings: Marshmallows, Whipped Cream, Chocolate Sauce
- The order card ticks off each part as you match it. Tap **Done** when the cocoa is ready.
- Match the order for 3 stars and a big tip. "Surprise me!" customers want it loaded with toppings. Be quick for a bonus.
- Eight customers a day, and orders get fancier each day.

Customers: Ariel, Sarah, Molly, Max, and whichever kids aren't making cocoa. Everyone is bundled up for the snow.

- `art.js` (the family and Max) and `kids.js` (Reuben, Jonah and Ellie) come from Ariel Passover Cookout,
  with winter clothes added: coats, snow pants, boots, mittens, scarves, hats and earmuffs.
- `cocoa.js` draws the cups, the hot chocolate, the toppings and the builder pictures.
- `game.js` draws the snowy stand and runs the game.
