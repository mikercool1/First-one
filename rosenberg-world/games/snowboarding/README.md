# 360 SNOWBOARDING

A 2.5D arcade snowboarding race down Coco Mountain, starring the Coco Party kids
(Reuben, Jonah, Ellie and Max, in their Coco Party winter clothes).

- Drag anywhere to carve left and right; a quick tap jumps (a second finger taps to jump while steering).
  Desktop: arrow keys and space, or mouse drag and click.
- In the air, drag left or right to spin (180, 360, 540...), tap to grab. Let go and you straighten
  out by yourself for a clean landing.
- Five sections: Starting Ridge, Pine Forest, Big Mountain, Ice Canyon and the Ski Village finish.
  Gates are +100, missing one is fine; crashes knock you down for a second, never restart the race.
- Eight cosmetic boards: Fire, Ice, Watermelon, Lightning, Galaxy, Shark, Gold and Coco.

Files: `game.js` (course, physics, rendering), `boards.js` (the boards), `riders.js` (the Coco Party
kids from `../coco-party/kids.js`, plus snowboard poses). Max is drawn by `../coco-party/art.js`.
